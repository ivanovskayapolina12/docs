### Account Registration

To get started, first create your credentials on our banking portal by navigating to banking.pagsmile.com.br. Once there, click on "Create a new account" and fill the form.

### Login

After you have registered, return to the login page and enter your phone number and password. Once you click "Login," the system will prompt you for an MFA code; if this is your first time, click on "I don't have a code" to configure your Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and set it up. For all subsequent logins, you will be required to provide your phone number, password, and the unique 6-digit verification code generated by the app to ensure your funds remain secure.

### **Onboarding (KYC/KYB)**

Once registered, log in to begin the mandatory verification process required to comply with Central Bank of Brazil regulations. For individual merchants or sole proprietors, you must complete the KYC (Personal Account) process by providing your full legal name, a verified email address, a clear photo of the front of your ID, a selfie holding that ID, and a valid proof of address showing your CEP. If you are registering as a legal entity or corporation, you must complete the KYB (Merchant Account) process, which requires your company’s legal name, a verified business email, an updated Company Registration Certificate (Contrato Social), and the company’s official registered business address.

#### 👤 KYC (Personal Accounts)

Best for individual merchants or sole proprietors. You will need:

* Full Legal Name & verified Email address.
* Photo ID: A clear photo of the front of your document.
* Selfie: A photo of yourself holding your ID.
* Proof of Address: A valid document showing your CEP (Zip Code).

#### 🏢 KYB (Merchant Accounts)

Required for legal entities and corporations. You will need:

* Company Legal Name & verified Company Email.
* Registration Documents: Updated Company Registration Certificate (*Contrato Social*).
* Business Address: Official registered address of the company.

> Note: Our Compliance team reviews all applications. In some cases, we may reach out via email for Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) if additional documentation is required.

### **Dashboard Access**

Once reviewed, you will have full access to the **Pagsmile Banking Dashboard.**&#x20;

#### Pro-Tips

* Language Support: You can toggle between Portuguese, English, and Chinese using the language selector in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
* Password Recovery: If you lose access, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset via your registered phone number.
* Image Quality: Ensure all document uploads are high-resolution and uncropped to avoid delays in approval.
