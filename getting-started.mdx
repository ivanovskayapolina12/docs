Set up your business account through the Pagsmile onboarding portal. This guide takes you from phone verification to company review and account activation.

**Your path:** Register → Verify your business and representatives → Submit for review → Activate your account.

## Before you begin

Keep your phone and email accessible throughout onboarding. Prepare the following information so you can complete each section without interruption.

| For your business | For each representative being verified |
| --- | --- |
| Full company name and CNPJ | Personal details and contact information |
| Business phone, email, and address | An identity document accepted by the verification form |
| Articles of Association (*Contrato Social*) or company details for manual entry | A camera for the selfie check |
| Business activities, website, incorporation date, and expected monthly processing volume | Residential address and proof of address |
| Supporting business documents and ownership details | Access to the verification session |

**KYB** (Know Your Business) checks your company's information and documents. **Identity verification** checks the individuals associated with the company. Both are part of this business onboarding flow.

## Account registration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your phone number">
    Open the [onboarding portal](https://banking-onboarding.pagsmile.com.br/onboarding) and enter your mobile phone number, including the country code.

    Review the consent text, select the required confirmation, and choose **Send Verification Code**. Enter the six-digit code sent to your phone to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your company details">
    Provide your full company name, CNPJ, and email address. Review the information and the consent statements, select the required confirmations, and choose **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start business verification">
    Choose **Start KYB Verification**, then **Start Verification**. In the verification window, select your preferred language and choose **Get Started**.

    The business verification process is provided through AIPrise and contains seven sections, described below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Complete business verification

Work through all seven sections in order. Use the details on your company records and review any information filled in automatically before continuing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Company information">
    Upload your **Articles of Association (Contrato Social)** to populate the company information form. If you do not have the document available, select **Click here to fill form manually** and enter the details yourself.

    Review the company information and complete all required fields. Manual entry replaces the automatic form filling; you must still provide the documents requested later in the application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business contact information">
    Enter your business phone number and email address, then choose **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business address">
    Enter your company's business address, including the country, street address, state, city, and postal code. Choose **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Economic and financial profile">
    Select your entity type, business model, and industry. Describe your main business activities, provide your website URL, select your expected monthly processing volume, and enter the company incorporation date.

    Review the completed profile and choose **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business documents">
    Upload the supporting documents requested in the form. These may include a bank account opening letter or statement, proof of business address, and a corporate structure chart. Follow the document types and file requirements shown for your application, then choose **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Corporate structure and legal representatives">
    Choose **Add Related Person**, complete the required information, and select **Save**. The form includes the person's role, name, date of birth, ownership information, phone number, and email.

    Next, choose **Verify Now** for the person and complete the [identity verification steps](#representative-identity-verification).

    <Warning>
      Saving a representative's details does not complete their verification. Complete the required identity checks before continuing to the next section.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Corporate shareholders">
    If your company has corporate shareholders, choose **Add Related Company** and complete the requested details. Review the application, then choose **Submit** to send it for review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Representative identity verification

After selecting **Verify Now**, choose **Get Started**, review the preparation screen, and select **I am ready**. Complete the five checks below.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Personal information** | Enter the requested personal details, then choose **Next**. |
| **2. Identity document** | Select the issuing country and an accepted document type. Photograph or upload the front and back when requested, and review the images before continuing. |
| **3. Selfie** | Choose **Take photo** and follow the on-screen instructions to capture your selfie. |
| **4. Address** | Enter your residential address and choose **Next**. |
| **5. Proof of address** | Select the document type, upload the requested proof of address, and choose **Submit**. |

<Tip>
  Use clear, readable document images with all required details visible. For the selfie, keep your face within the frame and follow the lighting and positioning guidance on screen.
</Tip>

After the identity verification request is submitted, return to the business application to complete any remaining sections. Submitting an identity check and submitting the full business application are separate steps.

## Resume your application

To continue an existing verification session or check its status:

1. Return to the onboarding verification flow and choose **Resume Existing Session** when prompted.
2. Enter the email address you used to start the session and choose **Send OTP**.
3. Enter the one-time password sent to that email and choose **Verify OTP**.
4. Review the application status and complete any outstanding steps.

<Note>
  The email verification code is valid for **10 minutes**. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder. This email code is separate from the phone verification code used during registration.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Update a representative's information" icon="pen">
    Open the representative's entry and edit the details. After changing submitted information, choose **Verify Now** to complete verification again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add or remove a related person" icon="user-plus">
    You can remove an existing entry or choose **Add Related Person** to enter someone else's details. Each newly added person must complete the required identity verification.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Review and account activation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for the application review">
    After you submit the complete business application, its status changes to **Under review**. The Pagsmile team checks the submitted information and documents.

    Monitor your email for updates and any requests for additional information. Registration or submission alone does not mean your application has been approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive approval and your activation email">
    Once approved, the application shows **Application Approved** and you receive an email notification. An activation email provides the link to set up your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your login credentials">
    Open the activation email and choose **Activate My Account**. On the **Set Your Login Credentials** page, complete the requested fields, create and confirm your password, and choose **Activate Account**.

    Follow the on-screen instructions to sign in to Pagsmile Banking.
  </Step>
</Steps>
