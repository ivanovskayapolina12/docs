MA is the Master Account. MOR and VA belong directly to MA. A PA's direct parent can be MA or a VA under the same MA. VA and PA cannot be created without a parent.

### **Capabilities and onboarding requirements by account type**

Once an account is active, its bank account is ready, and required services are enabled, it can retrieve balances and statements, manage Pix keys, and perform collections, payments, refunds, and dispute operations. Funds and transactions are recorded separately per account; hierarchy does not imply automatic balance aggregation.

VA and PA accounts do not need to resubmit the MA's business information, but must still provide their own entity information and complete the applicable compliance processes. VA currently supports account opening only for legal entities with a CNPJ. PA supports individuals with a CPF or legal entities with a CNPJ. For technical integration requirements and API call examples, see [Create managed account](#api-36).

      <td>Operate the MOR/VA bank account bound to the credentials</td>

    </tr>
  </tbody>

The parent account specified during account opening differs from the account whose funds are used for a transaction. To initiate a transaction for a PA, you must specify the PA's own bank account.

## **Integration examples**

### **Account models: choose by funds ownership**

Using product sales as an example, choose based on funds ownership and whether a separate account needs to be opened.

<table>
  <colgroup>
    <col width="130" />
    <col width="619" />
  </colgroup>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Business needsBusiness needs</th>
      <th>Selection guidance</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>A business sells its own products and collects payments centrally</td>
      <td>Complete MA integration first. Evaluate MOR if you need to reuse the MA's compliance records to create an operating account. Ordinary buyers do not need a PA.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Open separate accounts for merchants joining the platform</td>
      <td>Legal entities can evaluate VA. Individuals or legal entities can evaluate PA based on eligibility requirements.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Buyers need their own funds accounts</td>
      <td>Evaluate PA and separately confirm permissions for funds operations such as payments and withdrawals.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Account ownership is not the same as debit authorization. The final choice depends on entity eligibility and enabled services.
