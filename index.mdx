### Relationship Between Accounts and Payment Functions

- After opening an account, verify its compliance status and confirm that the bank account is ready before using the enabled account services and payment capabilities.
- Collections, payments, and account inquiries must use the appropriate authorization context for the target account. Account ownership and hierarchy do not imply automatic balance aggregation.
- Pix refunds must be linked to the original collection transaction. MED cases must also be linked to the original Pix transaction and meet their respective business requirements. The connections indicate business relationships, not call sequences or quantity relationships.
- Webhooks notify the business of changes based on subscribed event types and scopes. Use detail, status, and transaction record queries to confirm processing results. MED callbacks are subject to the enabled services and agreed delivery scope.

### Pagsmile Feature Overview

The Pagsmile Open API integrates account and payment services into your business system. Through the API, you can create managed accounts, retrieve bank account information, manage Pix Keys, initiate payments and refunds, and track processing results through query APIs and event notifications.

Business operations use POST requests with JSON request bodies and AK/SK signature authentication. Account permissions are determined by the credential type, account ownership, and target account.

### Intended Audience

The Open API is designed for businesses and platforms that need to manage accounts, payments, and transaction results within their own systems.

Integrators can manage their own primary accounts. With the appropriate permissions for managed accounts, they can also create Virtual Accounts (VAs) or Platform Accounts (PAs) for their business entities and manage each account's information and transaction records separately.

Account creation and management, fund operations, and event subscriptions have different authorization scopes. Before integration, configure the appropriate credentials based on the features you need to use.

    [Learn how to authenticate requests →](/integration-overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your payment workflow">
    Use the reference for your chosen operation, then implement status checks and webhook handling where applicable. Some operations finish asynchronously, so an accepted request may still be processing.

  </Step>
</Steps>
